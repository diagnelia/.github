**Diagnelia is an AI guide for people still searching for a diagnosis.** People with rare conditions often see doctor after doctor for years, and the pattern only shows when someone sees all of it. Diagnelia turns years of symptoms, visits and documents into one clear, sourced page that the next doctor can read in two minutes.

> **Stage:** in development. Diagnelia organises health information. It does not diagnose, treat or replace a doctor. In an emergency, call 112.

## What the AI does

- **Listens.** A calm, guided conversation at your pace: when it began, what makes it worse, family history.
- **Reads.** Letters, scans and lab reports are read, dated and placed in one timeline.
- **Orders.** One chronology, with gaps and unclear dates shown, never guessed.
- **Translates.** Your own words into the clinical terms doctors use, with every line linked to its source.
- **Prepares the next step.** Questions for your next visit, the kind of specialist who may help, and European expert centres to ask about.

## What the AI does not do

- It never names a disease to you. Diagnosis stays with your doctor.
- It does not replace your doctor. It prepares the visit, and the doctor decides.
- It stops at warning signs and tells you to call 112 or see a doctor today.
- It does not sell your data, and your story never trains an AI unless you say yes.
