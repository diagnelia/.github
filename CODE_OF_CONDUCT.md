# Code of conduct

Diagnelia is built for people who have often waited years to be heard. We expect everyone who takes part in our repositories, issues and discussions to treat others the same way.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent and keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Remember that patients, families and clinicians may read what you write.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- **Posting real personal or medical data**, your own or anyone else's. Use invented examples only.
- Giving medical advice to other people in our spaces.

## Medical emergencies

Diagnelia cannot help in an emergency, and neither can a GitHub issue. **If you or someone else needs urgent help, call 112** (the European emergency number) or your local emergency number.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email **info@diagnelia.com**. We read every report. We may edit or remove content, and may block people who repeatedly break these rules.
