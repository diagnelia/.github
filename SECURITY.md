# Security policy

Diagnelia will handle some of the most sensitive data there is, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **info@diagnelia.com** with the subject line **Security**.

Include, where you can:

- what is affected (a repository, the website diagnelia.com or another Diagnelia service)
- steps to reproduce, or a short proof of concept
- the impact you expect

**Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a vulnerability**, and do not include real personal or medical data in your report.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report.
- We look into it and keep you informed about what we find and what we fix.
- When it is fixed, we are happy to credit you, if you want that.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it publicly.
